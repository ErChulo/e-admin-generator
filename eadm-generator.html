Header data for the memo. It comes from PBGC case records, not from plan documents.

Step 2 — Download the scraper prompt

Feed the prompt and the documents to your LLM (Microsoft Copilot in the office, any LLM at home). The LLM returns a JSON proposal. It never writes the memo.

Documents to attach: plan documents of all eras (5A/5B/5C), the R5 Preliminary Plan Summary (12D1), PF factor sets per era (12E1/12E2/12E3), participant data (SPECTRUM download from TRMD), the Earnings Offsets Summary if any (§6), the Employee Contributions Processing Evaluation (TP 36) if any (§7), and the Actuarial Case Memo EPRD language (§2). Do not attach the §436 Evaluation or the DEL — they are not E.Adm inputs.

Step 3 — Upload JSON

Paste the JSON the LLM returned, or upload its file. The app validates it against the schema. Ambiguous or uncited answers are flagged, never auto-filled.

Check each section. Approve a section only when its answer and facts are right. Nothing generates until all 7 sections are approved.

Welcome

The E.Adm Generator writes the Estimated Benefits Administration Analysis memo. It finds the issues that affect how benefits are administered, and who should be in pay.

How it works, in 5 steps: case header · download the scraper prompt · feed the prompt and the documents to an LLM · upload the JSON the LLM returns · review each section · generate the Word memo.

The memo has 7 sections: Required Beginning Dates, Earliest PBGC Retirement Date, Retroactive QPSAs, Plan Adjustment Factors, Restrictions from another source, Earnings Offsets, Employee Contributions.

The LLM proposes. You review and approve. Only approved JSON writes the memo. The app never computes a benefit and never fetches PBGC systems — participant data comes from your upload.

Help

What the memo is

Template: Estimated Benefits Administration Analysis Memo, version of 09/18/2020. A plain memo to EBLS and the field office. Its purpose: to identify operating policies and issues that affect the administration of benefits under the plan.

The 7 sections

1. Required Beginning Dates. Policy 5.2-5, TP 20.2. Any participants past or within 180 days of their RBD who are not yet in pay? If yes, document the findings and instruct EBLS or the field office to send applications commencing on the RBD.

2. Earliest PBGC Retirement Date (EPRD). Policies 5.2-4 and 6.1-2. Four Yes/No questions: non-de minimis lump sums, annuities before age 55, early unreduced benefits, window periods. The EPRD language comes from the Actuarial Case Memo. Note: the template spells it “ERPD” in one line — that is the template’s typo, kept as written.

3. Retroactive QPSAs. Policies 5.2-4 and 5.7-2. Any beneficiaries entitled to QPSAs with annuity starting dates retroactive to dates before DOPT? If yes, document and instruct EBLS or the field office to send applications commencing retroactively.

4. Plan Adjustment Factors. Did earlier plan documents provide different Early Retirement, Late Retirement, or Form Conversion Factors than the DOPT document? If yes, document the effective dates and the IPS location, and apply the appropriate factors.

5. Restrictions from another source. Does the plan restrict benefits payable while participants receive certain benefits from another source? If yes, describe the provisions and give guidance for benefit payments.

6. Earnings Offsets. Policy 6.2-1, TP 51. Any benefit supplements subject to earnings offsets? If yes, attach or reference the Earnings Offsets Summary. Note: PBGC does not apply earnings offsets in estimated benefits — this section only identifies the supplements.

7. Employee Contributions. Policies 5.11-1 and 5.11-2, TP 36. If the plan has employee contributions, an Employee Contributions Processing Evaluation (TP 36) must be completed. Select the statements that apply; the rest are deleted.

What the app does not do

It does not compute RBDs, EPRDs, or benefit amounts. It does not fetch SPECTRUM, QuEST, or TRMD data. It does not ask for the §436 Evaluation or the DEL — they are not E.Adm inputs. Participant names and SSNs never go in the JSON; use participant refs only.

After the memo

Image the finished memo in Image Viewer, Index 9C. Notify the Auditor, EBLS, and FBA. Submit to the Actuarial Reviewer.
